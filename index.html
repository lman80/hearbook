HearBook Your books, read aloud. Free.

Lifelike voices that live right on your device, so every book you own can be read to you for free. No subscription, no credits, no internet needed.

Download for Mac

Listen

Tap a paragraph. It starts reading.

HearBook reads your ePubs, PDFs and documents in a lifelike voice that runs right on your device. No internet needed.

  • Follow along. Each sentence lights up and the page turns by itself.
  • Keeps going with the screen locked, with controls on your Lock Screen and a sleep timer for bedtime.
  • Easy on the eyes. Light, sepia or night pages, and fonts like Atkinson Hyperlegible and OpenDyslexic.

Remember

Say a thought. It’s pinned to the page.

Hold the microphone and talk. Let go, and your words become a written note, saved at the exact spot you were listening to.

  • Save a quote by pressing and holding any line.
  • One page per book keeps every note, quote and bookmark together.
  • Jump back to the passage behind any note with one tap.

Snap

Point at any page. Hear it.

Snap a letter, a menu, a label or a page of a paper book, and HearBook reads it to you. The words are recognised on your device.

  • Scan whole books. Pages are caught automatically, and an open book’s two pages are read in order.
  • Save what you snap to your library, or just listen and move on.
  • Your photos stay yours. Nothing is uploaded.
