SpeakBook for Mac

Free. Apple silicon Mac, macOS 15 or later. Not on the Mac App Store.

Download SpeakBook for Mac

Install

  1. Open SpeakBook-Mac.zip from your Downloads.
  2. Drag SpeakBook into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. It's signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

You don't need the Mac app to use SpeakBook. Everything works on your iPhone and iPad, free on the App Store. The Mac app adds extra voices, makes audiobooks, and syncs your books and notes with your other devices.
